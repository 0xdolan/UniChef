# Privacy

UniChef collects no data. This applies to the browser extensions, the web app, the command-line tool and the libraries.

- Text you inspect or transform is processed on your device and is never sent anywhere.
- There are no analytics, telemetry, crash reports, accounts or cookies.
- The browser extensions ask for no access to the pages you visit. They read a selection only when you choose "Inspect with UniChef" on it. Settings are kept in the browser's own extension storage, which your browser may sync between your devices if you have turned that on.
- The web app is static files on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log requests to it under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/0xdolan/unichef/issues.
